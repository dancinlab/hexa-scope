# Hubble Space Telescope (HST)

> Edwin Hubble · launched 1990-04-24 (STS-31) · 2.4 m primary mirror · low Earth orbit (~540 km, i=28.5°) · operational 36 years and counting.

## §1 Overview

NASA/ESA flagship optical/UV/near-IR space telescope. Five servicing missions
(SM1 1993 corrective optics — COSTAR; SM2 1997; SM3A/3B 1999/2002; SM4 2009)
extended the lifetime far beyond original 15-yr design. Foundational to modern
extragalactic astronomy: Hubble Deep Field (1995), Hubble Ultra Deep Field
(2003), eXtreme Deep Field (2012). Co-discovered dark energy via SNe Ia
(Riess/Perlmutter 1998). Cepheid distance ladder anchor for H₀ tension
(SH0ES H₀ ≈ 73 km/s/Mpc vs Planck CMB 67.4).

## §2 Mirror geometry

Single monolithic Cassegrain (Ritchey-Chrétien) primary, 2.4 m diameter,
hyperbolic f/24 Cassegrain configuration. Originally ground 2.2 µm
spherical-aberration deformity at the edge — corrected in SM1 (1993) by
COSTAR + WFPC2 with built-in corrective optics. Single-segment monolith =
**not** a hexagonal segmented mirror. Hubble represents the pre-segmented
design epoch; JWST is the n=6 inheritor.

## §3 Spectral band

- UV: 115-320 nm (STIS, COS, ACS/SBC)
- Optical: 200-1000 nm (WFC3/UVIS, ACS/WFC)
- Near-IR: 800-1700 nm (WFC3/IR, NICMOS retired)

Diffraction limit ~50 mas at 500 nm. Thermal IR (>2 µm) **not** Hubble's
domain — passed to JWST.
