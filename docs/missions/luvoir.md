# LUVOIR — Large UV/Optical/IR Surveyor (proposal lineage)

> Astro2020 Decadal proposal (study A: 15 m, study B: 8 m). Merged with HabEx into the **Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO)** as the formal post-Hubble flagship; launch target ~2040s. LUVOIR per se is **not** a flown mission — included as a proposal lineage anchor.

## §1 Overview

LUVOIR was the 2020 NASA decadal-survey concept study for a flagship UV/
optical/near-IR space telescope to succeed Hubble. Two architectures:
LUVOIR-A 15 m segmented at L2; LUVOIR-B 8 m segmented. Astro2020 Pathways
to Discovery merged the LUVOIR + HabEx scopes into a 6 m-class IR/O/UV
**Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO)** with ExoEarth Survey science case
as the formal Great Observatory after JWST/Roman. Serviceability (in-space
robotic upgrade) is an HWO design requirement.

## §2 Mirror geometry

Both LUVOIR-A (15 m, 120 hex segments) and LUVOIR-B (8 m, 55 hex segments)
inherit the **JWST hexagonal-segment lattice paradigm** — same n=6 hex
tile, scaled. HWO (current 6 m descendant) is expected to retain hex
segmentation. The hex segment is the *de facto* standard for all
post-JWST flagship UV/optical/IR space telescopes (cf. Keck on the
ground: 36 hex segments).

| concept   | diameter | segments | segment type     |
|-----------|----------|----------|------------------|
| LUVOIR-A  | 15.0 m   | 120      | hexagonal        |
| LUVOIR-B  |  8.0 m   |  55      | hexagonal        |
| HWO (cur) |  ~6.0 m  | ~37      | hexagonal (TBD)  |

## §3 Spectral band

- LUVOIR FUV: 100-200 nm (LUMOS multi-object spec)
- NUV: 200-400 nm (POLLUX UV spec, polarimetry)
- Optical: 400-1000 nm (HDI imager, ECLIPS coronagraph)
- Near-IR: 1.0-2.5 µm (HDI, ECLIPS)

UV is the **defining gap** Hubble (STIS/COS) currently fills; HWO is the
only credible post-Hubble UV substrate.
