# Origins Space Telescope (proposal)

> Astro2020 Decadal far-IR / sub-mm flagship concept; not selected as the top decadal priority (HWO won). Origins remains a probe-class concept lineage and the long-pole far-IR successor to Herschel/Spitzer.

## §1 Overview

Origins was the 2020 decadal far-IR/sub-mm flagship concept study, intended
as a successor to Herschel (2009-2013) and Spitzer (2003-2020) at IR
wavelengths beyond JWST/MIRI's 28 µm cutoff. Science cases: water trail in
protoplanetary disks (snow line), galaxy assembly through obscured star
formation (z=1-6), exoplanet atmospheres in the mid-IR. Astro2020 did not
recommend Origins as the next flagship — the decadal report instead routed
the budget toward HWO; Origins continues as a **probe-class** descendant
concept.

## §2 Mirror geometry

5.9 m primary (cooled to 4.5 K via active cryocoolers — radically colder
than JWST's MIRI 6.7 K). Two architectures studied:
- **Concept 1**: 9 m off-axis open-back primary
- **Concept 2 (baseline)**: 5.9 m on-axis 3-mirror anastigmat with hex
  segmentation (similar topology to LUVOIR)

Hex segmentation candidate; final architecture not selected (concept frozen
at decadal end).

## §3 Spectral band

- 2.8 - 36 µm (mid-IR; MISC instrument suite)
- 25 - 588 µm (far-IR; OSS spectrometer)
- 50 - 230 µm (FIP polarimetric imager)

The 25-588 µm band is the **spectral gap** between JWST/MIRI and ground
sub-mm (ALMA 350 µm-3 mm) that no current space mission covers.

## §5 F-gate structure

- **F-ORIGINS-1** — Active 4.5 K cryocooler tech matured to TRL 6 by
  2030; falsified at TRL <5 by 2028 review.
- **F-ORIGINS-2** — Concept survives as probe-class mission ($1B class)
  by 2032; falsified if descoped to <$500M (would lose far-IR capability).
- **F-ORIGINS-3** — Sub-mm gap (Herschel ended 2013) closed before 2045;
  falsified if no 25-500 µm space mission flies in 2013-2045 (32+ yr gap).
- **F-ORIGINS-4** — Water snow-line detection in 100+ protoplanetary
  disks demonstrated on alternative facility (e.g. JWST/MIRI MRS at <28 µm
  partial coverage); falsified if water trail science remains
  observationally inaccessible.

Status: **decadal-ranked but not selected**; live as probe concept.
